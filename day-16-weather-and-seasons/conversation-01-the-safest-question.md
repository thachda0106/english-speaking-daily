# Day 16 · Conversation 1 — The Safest Question In English

## 🎬 Situation
Someone mentions the weather. This is the most reliable small talk in the world — you can always start here, and nobody minds. Most people just don't take the opportunity.

## 🗣️ Dialogue

> **Friend:** God, look at it out there.

> **You:** Yeah — I thought it was supposed to rain this afternoon.

> **Friend:** It did. About an hour ago. I got completely soaked.

> **You:** Classic. It always rains exactly when you don't take a coat.

> **Friend:** I never learn.

> **You:** Same here. I checked the forecast this morning and decided it was lying.

> **Friend:** It's nice now, though. You can smell it after.

> **You:** That's the best part. I should probably go outside while it's fine.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary

- **look at it out there** — the standard way to open a weather conversation. **"it"** does all the work
- **I thought it was supposed to rain** — past tense for a prediction that failed. Very common
- **It always rains exactly when you don't take a coat** — a general truth. *"always"* and *"exactly"* make it funny
- **You can smell it after** — a genuinely nice observation, and the kind that turns small talk into conversation

## ✅ After you speak

**Formula:** *Yeah — I thought it was supposed to ___. — It always ___ exactly when you ___. — You can smell it after.*

Weather is a gift: **it needs no knowledge, no opinions, and no risk.** Everybody has an opinion about the rain. Practise three turns and you'll never be stuck again.

## ✅ Done when…
- …you can open with *"look at it out there"* or *"God, look at the sky"*
- …you can complain about the weather without sounding boring
- …you can take it past the weather into something real 🎯
