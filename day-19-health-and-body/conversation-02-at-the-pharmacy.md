# Day 19 · Conversation 2 — At The Pharmacy

## 🎬 Situation
You need medicine and you have to explain it to a stranger who is not a doctor. There is a standard shape, and it works every time.

## 🗣️ Dialogue

> **Pharmacist:** Hello. What can I help you with?

> **You:** Hi — I've got a headache and a temperature. I don't know what to take.

> **Pharmacist:** Right. Have you taken anything today?

> **You:** No, I wanted to ask first.

> **Pharmacist:** Okay. Anything else? Allergies?

> **You:** No, none I know of.

> **Pharmacist:** Take this twice a day, with food. And don't mix it with ibuprofen.

> **You:** Okay. How long until it works?

> **Pharmacist:** Usually about half an hour. If you're not better in three days, see your doctor.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary

- **What can I help you with?** — the pharmacist's opener. Not *"what's wrong?"*, which is more dramatic
- **have you taken anything today?** — asking what medicine you've already had. Have the answer ready
- **allergies?** — one word is enough. If you have any, say what they are: *"I'm allergic to penicillin"*
- **don't mix it with ibuprofen** — **"mix it with"** means take two medicines together. Very useful and very common

## ✅ After you speak

**Formula:** *I've got ___ and ___. I don't know what to take. — Anything else? Allergies? — How long until it works?*

⚠️ **Always answer the allergy question, even if you have none.** *"No, none I know of"* is a complete, natural answer. Silence looks like you didn't hear.

## ✅ Done when…
- …you can explain a problem in three short sentences
- …you can answer *"allergies?"* without hesitating
- …you can ask *"how long until it works?"* and *"can I take this with ___?"* 🎯
