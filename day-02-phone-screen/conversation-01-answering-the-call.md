# Day 2 · Conversation 1 — Answering the Call

## 🎬 Situation
Your phone rings at 7:00 PM. It's a recruiter calling about the backend job you applied for. Phone calls are the hardest to understand — but also the most common first step.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles), then play the audio and answer

> **Recruiter:** Hello, is this Thach Nguyen?

> **You:** Yes, this is Thach.

> **Recruiter:** Hi Thach, this is Maria from TechStar Company. Thank you for applying for our backend position. Do you have a few minutes to talk now?

> **You:** Oh, hello Maria! Yes, I have time. Thank you for calling me.

> **Recruiter:** Great. I read your CV, and it looks very good.

> **You:** Thank you, I'm happy to hear that.

> **Recruiter:** Before we start — is this a good time to talk?

> **You:** Yes, it is. Please go ahead.

> **Recruiter:** Perfect. Let's begin.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary — all words you already know
`phone · call · position · minutes · time · CV · looks · good · happy · hear · start · begin`

## ✅ After you speak
- 💡 **"Yes, this is Thach."** is the correct phone answer. NOT "Yes, I am Thach" — on the phone we say *"this is…"*, never *"I am…"*. Say it 5 times out loud.
- 💡 **"Is this a good time to talk?"** is THE polite way to start any call — use it yourself when calling a colleague.
- 💡 If the call comes at a bad moment: **"I'm a bit busy right now. Could I call you back in ten minutes?"** — say this one out loud too.
