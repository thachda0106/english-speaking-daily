# Day 2 · Conversation 3 — Your Experience & Why You're Leaving

## 🎬 Situation
The recruiter asks about your current job and — the uncomfortable question — why you want to leave. Prepare the honest answer BEFORE the call; never improvise this one.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles), then play the audio and answer

> **Recruiter:** I have a few quick questions. Can you tell me about your current job?

> **You:** Sure. I'm a backend developer at a software company. I've been there for about two years.

> **Recruiter:** And what part of your job do you enjoy the most?

> **You:** I enjoy solving problems — especially when a system is slow or has a bug. I like finding the reason and fixing it.

> **Recruiter:** That's a great answer. And why are you looking for a new job?

> **You:** I want to grow and work on bigger systems. My current company is a good place, but the projects are small.

> **Recruiter:** I understand. A lot of developers change jobs for the same reason.

> **You:** Yes, exactly. I'm looking for a new challenge.

> **Recruiter:** I see on your CV that you know AWS. Do you use it at work?

> **You:** Yes — my team deploys on AWS ECS, and I write Terraform for our infrastructure.

> **Recruiter:** That's very useful experience.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary — all words you already know
`questions · current · job · backend · developer · software · company · years · enjoy · solving · problems · system · slow · bug · finding · reason · fixing · looking · new · grow · bigger · projects · small · developers · change · challenge · AWS · ECS · Terraform · infrastructure · experience`

## ✅ After you speak
- This is your **"why leave" script**: *grow → bigger systems → current projects are small → new challenge.* Never say bad things about your company — this answer is honest AND professional.
- 💡 **"I've been there for about two years."** — use *there* (not "here"!) because the company is a different place from where you're talking now. Small word, big difference.
- 💡 Mentioning AWS + Terraform to a recruiter is a **power move** — it sells your value in one line. Keep that answer exactly.
