# Day 2 · Conversation 5 — Next Steps & Closing

## 🎬 Situation
The call is almost over. Now you need to know the process and close politely. NEVER end a call without knowing when you'll hear back.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles), then play the audio and answer

> **Recruiter:** I think you're a great fit for this position. Let me explain the next steps.

> **You:** Great, please go ahead.

> **Recruiter:** First, I'll send your CV to the technical team. If they like it, we'll book a technical interview for next week.

> **You:** That sounds good. Should I prepare anything for the interview?

> **Recruiter:** Just relax and review your basics — algorithms, SQL, and system design would be a good start.

> **You:** Got it, I'll review those. When can I expect to hear from you?

> **Recruiter:** You should hear from us within two or three days.

> **You:** Perfect. Thank you so much for your time, Maria. I really appreciate you taking the time to talk with me today.

> **Recruiter:** You're welcome, Thach. Have a great day!
