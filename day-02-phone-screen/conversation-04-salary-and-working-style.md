# Day 2 · Conversation 4 — Salary & Working Style

## 🎬 Situation
The scary question: "What are your salary expectations?" Recruiters expect a number — silence is worse than a wrong number. Prepare your range BEFORE the call.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles), then play the audio and answer

> **Recruiter:** Now, one more question. What are your salary expectations?

> **You:** My current salary is around twenty million VND. I'm looking for something higher — around twenty-five million, if possible.

> **Recruiter:** That sounds reasonable for your experience. I'll write that in your profile.

> **You:** Thank you. Also, I'm happy to work in the office or remotely — both are fine for me.

> **Recruiter:** Good to know. Our team works in a hybrid way: two days from home, three days in the office.

> **You:** That works for me.

> **Recruiter:** Perfect. Do you have any other questions about the role?

> **You:** I don't think so, for now. Everything sounds good.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary — all words you already know
`question · salary · expectations · current · million · looking · higher · possible · reasonable · experience · profile · office · remotely · fine · team · hybrid · home · role · everything`

## ✅ After you speak
- Your salary answer follows one clean formula: **current → higher → number.** Change the numbers to YOUR real ones before practicing.
- 💡 **"That works for me."** — the simplest natural agreement in English. Works for salary, schedules, deadlines, everything.
- 💡 **"for now"** — an honest, useful limiter: *"I don't have questions for now."* / *"This is fine for now."*
- 🎙️ Play `recruiter-04`. Say the salary answer 3 times — it must come out without thinking. Then say it with YOUR real numbers.
