# Day 8 · Conversation 4 — Agreeing on a New Date

## 🎬 Situation
Your manager says yes to a new date. Now the skill is the opposite one: **commit and be specific**. A new date is a promise, so you close it clearly — what, who, and when.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles), then play the audio and answer

> **Manager:** Okay, I agree we can move the date. Let's say the end of next month.

> **You:** Thank you. Can I confirm one thing — the first version is still next Wednesday, right?

> **Manager:** Yes, the download stays on Wednesday. The full page moves.

> **You:** Perfect. So I'll finish the download this week, and the page before the end of the month.

> **Manager:** That sounds good. What do you need from me?

> **You:** Two things: clear access to the test data, and a review from Duy on Wednesday.

> **Manager:** I can help with both. I'll send you the access today.

> **You:** Great, thank you. One more thing — should I tell the customer team about the new date? I'll send you a short update every Friday, so you always know where we are.

> **Manager:** I like that. Let's do it that way. And Thach — thank you for telling me early. It helps me a lot.

> **You:** Of course. It's easier to move a date now than to fix a broken promise on the last day.

## ✅ After you speak
- The new-date formula = **thank + confirm what stays + repeat the two dates + say what you need**:
  *"Thank you. So the first version is still Wednesday, and the full page is before the end of the month. I need test data access and a review from Duy."*
- 💡 **"Can I confirm one thing?"** — repeats the agreement in your own words. This is how you avoid a different understanding later.
- 💡 **"a short update every Friday"** — a small, regular promise. It makes you easy to work with, and it protects you if something goes wrong.
- 🧠 **Your mental model:** agreeing a new date = a new SLA. You restate the target, the owner, and the check-in — same three things you write in a design doc. Vague agreements rot; written ones don't.
- 🎙️ Play `manager-04` and answer. Then agree ONE new date with a real person and confirm it out loud in the same call.
