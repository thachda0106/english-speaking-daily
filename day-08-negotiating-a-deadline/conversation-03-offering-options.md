# Day 8 · Conversation 3 — Offering Options

## 🎬 Situation
You've read the code and you know the date is too short. Now comes the hard part: instead of complaining, you bring **choices**. A good engineer doesn't say "I can't" — they say "here are three ways."

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles), then play the audio and answer

> **You:** I read the code and I have good news and bad news. The good news: I know how to build it.

> **Manager:** Okay, and the bad news?

> **You:** The full task is too big for next Wednesday. I think we have three options. Do you have ten minutes?

> **Manager:** Yes, of course. Tell me the options.

> **You:** Option one: we keep the date and send only the download. Option two: we do everything, but the date moves one week. Option three: I need one more person for three days.

> **Manager:** That's clear, thank you. Which one do you think is the best?

> **You:** I think option one is the safest. The customers get the important part early, and we keep our promise on the date.

> **Manager:** Good thinking. Let's do option one, and we can add the page next week.

> **You:** Great, I agree. I'll update the task and the board today, and I'll write down why we chose it.

> **Manager:** Perfect. A short note like that helps the next person a lot.

## ✅ After you speak
- The options formula = **good news / bad news + three options + your recommendation**:
  *"I have good news and bad news. Here are three options… I think option one is the safest, and I recommend it."*
- 💡 **"Do you have ten minutes?"** — asking for time makes you look prepared, not difficult.
- 💡 **"I think option one is the safest."** — don't just present choices; recommend one. A recommendation is a decision you own.
- 🧠 **Your mental model:** three options = **do less work, take more time, or take more people**. You cannot make a sprint faster by wanting to. You change one of the three inputs, and you say out loud which one you changed.
- 🎙️ Play `manager-03` and answer. Then write THREE real options for a REAL task and send them to your manager in writing.
