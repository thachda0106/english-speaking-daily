# Day 8 · Conversation 2 — Asking Questions About the Scope

## 🎬 Situation
Before you estimate anything, you need to know exactly what "done" means. Asking good questions is not slow — it is the fastest way to a correct number. You learn to separate the parts you know from the parts you don't.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles), then play the audio and answer

> **Manager:** Hi Thach, about the report feature — can you start it today?

> **You:** Thanks. Before I start, can I ask a few questions about the scope?

> **Manager:** Sure, ask whatever you need.

> **You:** First — do the customers want the file as a spreadsheet or as a PDF?

> **Manager:** A spreadsheet. That's the important one for them.

> **You:** Okay. And do they need only the download, or a new page too?

> **Manager:** Both, actually. The page and the download.

> **You:** Okay. And the old data — two years of orders, or only this year?

> **Manager:** Only this year for the first version. Is there another team working on this part?

> **You:** No, it's all our team. I understand the task now — I'll make a list today, send it to you, and update the board.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary — all words you already know
`thanks · task · start · questions · scope · ask · screen · need · page · both · download · okay · old data · show · two years · orders · only · this year · first version · good · helps · question · another team · part · all · Duy · review · understand · list · today · update · board`

## ✅ After you speak
- The scope-question formula = **before I start + what does "done" include + what is not in the first version**:
  *"Before I start, can I ask about the scope? Do they need the page too, or only the download? Is two years of data in the first version?"*
- 💡 **"Before I start, can I ask a few questions?"** — the most professional sentence in this whole day. It says: I take this seriously, and I want the number to be right.
- 💡 **"Is that in the first version, or later?"** — separates the real deadline from the nice-to-have. Same trick as a "must / should / could" ticket.
- 🧠 **Your mental model:** questions are your requirements gathering. An unasked question becomes a hidden requirement, and hidden requirements are why estimates are wrong. Same reason you never ship without reading the log.
- 🎙️ Play `manager-02` and answer. Then ask THREE scope questions about a REAL ticket before you estimate it.
