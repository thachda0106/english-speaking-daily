# Day 8 · Conversation 1 — The Task Is Too Big

## 🎬 Situation
Your manager gives you a new task and a date. The date feels too short. The skill: don't say yes and disappear — buy time with a question, and be honest before you start.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles), then play the audio and answer

> **Manager:** Hi Thach. I have a new task for you. We need a report feature for the customers.

> **You:** Thanks, I'd be happy to help. Could you tell me more about it?

> **Manager:** Customers want to see all their orders in one place, and they want to download a file. I need it next Wednesday.

> **You:** Okay. Can I ask — is the date fixed, or can we talk about it?

> **Manager:** That's from a customer, so it's quite important. But I can ask for one more week if needed.

> **You:** Thank you. That helps a lot. Can I look at the code first and give you a real estimate tomorrow?

> **Manager:** Yes, take your time. The important part is that the number is right, not fast.

> **You:** That's exactly what I want to do. I'd rather be honest now than sorry on Tuesday.

> **Manager:** That makes sense. Tell me what you find and we'll decide then. Is there anything you need from me first?

> **You:** Perfect. I'll read the code this afternoon, update the task on the board, and come back to you tomorrow morning.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary — all words you already know
`new · task · report · feature · customers · show · orders · place · download · file · next · important · customer · fixed · talk · asked · one more week · needed · help · look · code · first · real · estimate · tomorrow · find · decide · update · board · afternoon · perfect · needed · honest`

## ✅ After you speak
- The too-big-task formula = **thank + ask if the date is fixed + ask for time to estimate**:
  *"Thank you. Can I look at the code first and give you a real estimate tomorrow?"*
- 💡 **"Is the date fixed, or can we talk about it?"** — the safest first question. It never says no; it opens the door.
- 💡 **"I can ask for one more week if needed."** — managers often WANT you to tell them. This sentence gives them permission to help.
- 🧠 **Your mental model:** a date you accept without reading the code is an unmeasured query plan. You don't know the cost until you EXPLAIN. Reading first = measuring first.
- 🎙️ Play `manager-01` and answer. Then find one REAL task on your board this week and ask your manager the date question out loud.
