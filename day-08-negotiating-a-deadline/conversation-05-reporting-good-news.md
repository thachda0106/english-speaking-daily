# Day 8 · Conversation 5 — Reporting Good News

## 🎬 Situation
You negotiated early, so now you can deliver early. The skill: report the good news the same way you reported the risk — with numbers and a clear next step. Early good news buys trust for the next negotiation.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles), then play the audio and answer

> **You:** Hi, I have some good news about the report feature.

> **Manager:** Good, tell me.

> **You:** I finished the first version today — two days early. The download works, and all the tests pass.

> **Manager:** That's great. Are all the parts finished, or only the first version?

> **You:** Only the first version. The page is about half done. I think I can finish it next Friday.

> **Manager:** Better than the old date. What's left to do on your side?

> **You:** Two things: I need a review from Duy, and I need the real data to test the file with.

> **Manager:** Okay, I'll ask Duy to review today. I'll send the data tomorrow morning.

> **You:** Thank you. I'll update the task on the board and send you a short update on Friday.

> **Manager:** Great work, Thach. This is exactly the update I need. Have a good day!

## ✅ After you speak
- The good-news formula = **headline + number + what's still open + what you need**:
  *"I finished the first version today — two days early. The page is half done, and I think I can finish it next Friday. I need a review from Duy."*
- 💡 **"two days early"** — one number makes the whole report believable. Always bring the number.
- 💡 **"Are all the parts finished, or only the first version?"** — managers ask this to check your honesty. Answer clearly, every time. Never let them think all of it is done when it isn't.
- 🧠 **Your mental model:** you spent Day 8 buying information and now you spend it paying it back. A deadline you discussed early turns into a date you beat early — that's how trust is built, exactly like a good on-call rotation: no news is bad news, and early news is a gift.
