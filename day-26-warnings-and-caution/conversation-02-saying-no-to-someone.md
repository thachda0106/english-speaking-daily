# Day 26 · Conversation 2 — Saying No To Someone

## 🎬 Situation
Someone asks you to do something and you've decided not to. This is different from Day 23's *"I can't today"* — that was about time. This is about the thing itself.

## 🗣️ Dialogue

> **Friend:** I need a sponsor for the event. Would you do it? I know it's a lot.

> **You:** No. I'm not going to sponsor it.

> **Friend:** Really? You don't have to say yes straight away.

> **You:** I know. I've thought about it — it's not about today. I just don't want to be a sponsor.

> **Friend:** Okay. Can I ask why?

> **You:** Sure. I don't mind supporting it, I just don't want my name on it. That's all.

> **Friend:** That's completely fine. Sorry to drag it out of you.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary

- **I don't want to be a sponsor** — **"I don't want to"** rather than *"I can't"*. If you say *"I can't"*, you invite a solution. If you say *"I don't want to"*, the conversation is about you
- **it's not about today** — clarifying that the answer isn't a timing issue. Very useful and often needed
- **I don't mind supporting it** — **"I don't mind"** contrasts with *"I don't want to"*. One is about effort, one is about principle
- **sorry to drag it out of you** — **"drag it out of"** means *extract reluctantly*. Exactly right for a no

## ✅ After you speak

**Formula:** *No. I'm not going to ___. — It's not about today. I just don't want to ___. — I don't mind ___, I just don't want ___.

⚠️ **"Can't" vs "don't want to"** is the most important distinction in this whole conversation:

| Saying | What the other person hears | Result |
|---|---|---|
| *I can't* | a problem with capacity | they'll try to solve it |
| *I'm not able to* | a scheduling issue | you'll be asked again |
| *I don't want to* | a decision about you | the conversation moves on |

**Say which one is true.** Only the third one is final.

## ✅ Done when…
- …you can refuse a request in one flat sentence
- …you can say *"it's not about today"* when it isn't
- …you can distinguish *"I can't"* from *"I don't want to"* 🎯
