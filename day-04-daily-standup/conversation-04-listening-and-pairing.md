# Day 4 · Conversation 4 — Listening & Offering to Pair

## 🎬 Situation
A teammate speaks too fast or you miss a name. Stand-ups are fast — asking for one repeat is normal and respectful. Then show initiative: offer to work together to learn.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles), then play the audio and answer

> **Colleague A:** And I'll finish the report service today if nothing breaks.

> **You:** Sorry, could you say that once more? I didn't quite catch the last part.

> **Colleague A:** Sure — the report service, the one that sends the daily emails.

> **Team lead:** Thach, is everything clear now?

> **You:** Almost — I just want to be sure about the service. If you need help with it, I'd like to pair with you, so I can learn the code.

> **Colleague A:** Sure, that would be great. We can work together after stand-up.

> **You:** Perfect. I'll finish my task first, then join you.

> **Team lead:** Great teamwork. Okay, let's continue.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary — all words you already know
`finish · report · service · breaks · sorry · catch · part · sure · emails · clear · almost · need · help · pair · learn · code · teamwork · work together · continue`

## ✅ After you speak
- 💡 **"Could you say that once more?"** — the polite repeat ask (softer than "What?"). Use it every time you miss something.
- 💡 **"pair with you"** = work together on one computer — the fastest way to learn a new codebase. Volunteer for it, it makes you look great.
- 💡 **"if nothing breaks"** = "if no problem happens" — very natural daily-work English.
- 🎙️ Play `standup-04` and answer. Then practice: when you miss something in a real meeting, use *"Could you say that once more?"*
