# Day 4 · Conversation 5 — Ending the Stand-up & After-Talk

## 🎬 Situation
The stand-up ends. Quick questions go to the lead privately after the meeting — never hold the whole team. Then a friendly goodbye before the workday starts.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles), then play the audio and answer

> **Team lead:** Okay, I think that's everyone. Anybody have anything else?

> **You:** Just one thing — I have a short question about the sprint board. Can we talk after the meeting?

> **Team lead:** Sure, come find me in ten minutes.

> **You:** Perfect, see you then. Thanks everyone, have a good day!

> **Colleague B:** You too, Thach. Don't forget lunch at noon!

> **You:** Ha, I won't. See you all later.

> **Team lead:** Alright, let's get to work!

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary — all words you already know
`everyone · anything · short · question · sprint · board · talk · meeting · minutes · see you · thanks · good day · forget · lunch · noon · later · alright · get to work · have a good day`

## ✅ After you speak
- 💡 "**Anybody have anything else?**" — the standard stand-up closing. Answer with *"Just one thing…"* if you have something, or *"Nope, that's all from me."*
- 💡 **"See you then."** / **"See you later."** / **"See you tomorrow."** — three time-words for goodbye, all natural.
- 💡 **"Don't forget lunch at noon!"** — small talk gold: *"don't forget"* is the everyday way to remind someone.
