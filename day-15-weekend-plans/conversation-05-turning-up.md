# Day 15 · Conversation 5 — Turning Up

## 🎬 Situation
You arrive somewhere. This is short and people hate it, but there's a formula and it makes you easy to be around.

## 🗣️ Dialogue

> **Friend:** Hiya!

> **You:** Hey — sorry, I'm late. Train was cancelled.

> **Friend:** Ugh, again?

> **You:** I'm honestly shocked it still runs. Where did you end up?

> **Friend:** Over by the entrance. You missed me by about a minute.

> **You:** Classic. Is this the place you were talking about?

> **Friend:** Yeah — have you been here before?

> **You:** No, first time. What should I get?

> **Friend:** Honestly, everything's good. Just start with the small plates.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary

- **Hiya / Hey** — informal hellos. **"Hiya"** is warm; **"hey"** is neutral
- **Sorry, I'm late — train was cancelled** — arrive with the reason already attached, so nobody has to ask
- **I missed you by about a minute** — from the other side, meaning you were almost together. Symmetry makes a small lateness feel small
- **What should I get?** — the question that makes you easy to feed. Genuinely useful

## ✅ After you speak

**Formula:** *Hey — sorry, I'm late. [reason]. — Where did you end up? — What should I get?*

Three sentences to arrive: **hello → reason → question.** The question is the important part. Somebody who arrives with a question is easy to include; somebody who arrives silent is work.

## ✅ Done when…
- …you can arrive with a reason attached and no long apology
- …you can find the other person with *"where did you end up?"*
- …you can ask *"what should I get?"* and let someone else lead 🎯
