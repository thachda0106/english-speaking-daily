# Day 27 · Conversation 1 — The First Hour

## 🎬 Situation
You start at a new company. The first hour decides how the morning feels. You need three small sentences that work every time: hello, one question, and how to find where things are.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles), then play the audio and answer

> **Colleague:** Morning! You must be the new person. I'm Ken.

> **You:** Hi Ken, nice to meet you. I'm Thach. Is this your first time here too?

> **Colleague:** No, I've been here two years. Let me show you where to put your things.

> **You:** Thanks, that would help. Where do we eat?

> **Colleague:** There's a kitchen on this floor, and a small shop near the station.

> **You:** Perfect. I'm a little lost, so that helps a lot.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary — all words you already know
`morning · new · person · first · here · show · where · put · things · thanks · help · where do we eat · kitchen · floor · small · shop · near · station · perfect · a little · lost · that helps`

## ✅ After you speak
- The first-hour formula = **hello + one question + ask where**:
  *"Hi, nice to meet you. Is this your first time here too? Where do we eat?"*
- 💡 **"You must be the new person."** — a natural way to start with a stranger. It's warmer than *"Are you new here?"* 🎯
- 💡 **"Is this your first time here too?"** — the *too* matters. It turns a question into a connection. Use it on day one with everyone.
- 💡 **"I'm a little lost."** — the most useful sentence for your first week. It's honest, small, and people immediately help you. Never pretend you understand when you don't.
- 🎙️ Play `colleague-01` and answer. **Today: say "I'm a little lost" out loud to yourself.** It has to be easy to say.
- 🧠 **Your mental model:** you already did this day in **Day 3**. This is the same day, said easier. Notice how much simpler it is now — because Days 11-26 made the words automatic.
