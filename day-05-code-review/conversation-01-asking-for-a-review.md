# Day 5 · Conversation 1 — Asking for a Review

## 🎬 Situation
Your code is ready. Before merging, every change needs a review. Asking the right way matters: give context, keep changes small, and say thank you.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles), then play the audio and answer

> **Colleague:** Hey Thach, how's the task going?

> **You:** It's going well. The fix is ready, and I created a pull request. Could you review it when you have time?

> **Colleague:** Sure, I can look at it after lunch. Is it a big change?

> **You:** Not really — about a hundred lines, plus some tests. I tried to keep it small.

> **Colleague:** Nice, small changes are easier to review. I'll take a look this afternoon.

> **You:** Thank you, I appreciate it. Let me know if anything is unclear.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary — all words you already know
`task · going well · fix · ready · created · pull request · review · time · change · hundred · lines · tests · small · easier · afternoon · appreciate · unclear`

## ✅ After you speak
- The review-request formula = **what's ready + ask + make it easy**:
  *"The fix is ready and I created a pull request. Could you review it when you have time?"*
- 💡 **"Let me know if anything is unclear."** — the perfect closer for any request. Use it in chats, emails, and meetings.
- 💡 **"I tried to keep it small."** — reviewers love this sentence. It shows you respect their time.
- 🎙️ Play `colleague-01` and answer. Then ask a REAL teammate for a review using the formula.
