# Day 5 · Conversation 5 — The Merge & Thanks

## 🎬 Situation
Approved! Your first merged change. The small rituals matter: confirm when to merge, close the task, and leave the team with a warm goodbye.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles), then play the audio and answer

> **Colleague:** Hey, I approved your pull request!

> **You:** Really? That's great news. Thank you for the careful review.

> **Colleague:** No problem. The changes look clean now. You can merge it when you're ready.

> **You:** Perfect. Should I merge it now, or is there a better time?

> **Colleague:** Anytime is fine — we merge as soon as a review passes.

> **You:** Great, I'll merge it and update the task on the board.

> **Colleague:** Nice work on your first feature!

> **You:** Thanks! It feels good. See you at the stand-up tomorrow!

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary — all words you already know
`approved · pull request · news · careful · review · changes · clean · merge · ready · better · time · anytime · fine · as soon as · passes · update · task · board · nice work · feature · feels good · stand-up · tomorrow`

## ✅ After you speak
- 💡 **"as soon as a review passes"** = immediately after approval. *"We deploy as soon as the tests pass."* — a useful phrase in CI/CD talk too.
- 💡 **"Nice work!"** — the standard praise at work. Answer: *"Thanks!"* — short and warm, nothing more needed.
- 💡 **"It feels good."** — a natural way to share satisfaction. *"I merged it — it feels good."*
