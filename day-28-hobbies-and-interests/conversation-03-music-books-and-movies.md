# Day 28 · Conversation 3 — Music, Books and Movies

## 🎬 Situation
The most common hobby conversation in the world. These are easy topics, which makes them good practice — you already know what a conversation feels like, so you can concentrate on the language.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles), then play the audio and answer

> **Friend:** What kind of music do you listen to?

> **You:** A lot of different kinds. I like slow songs when I'm tired, and fast music when I work.

> **Friend:** That's a good rule. I listen to music all day. What about movies?

> **You:** I don't watch many movies. I read more, mostly books about technology.

> **Friend:** Books are hard for me. I get tired after two pages.

> **You:** Same here, actually. I read one page three times before I understand it.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary — all words you already know
`kind · music · listen · different · slow songs · tired · fast music · work · good rule · all day · movies · watch many · read · mostly · books · technology · hard · get tired · two pages · same here · read one page three times · understand`

## ✅ After you speak
- The taste formula = **broad answer + when you use each one + contrast**:
  *"I like slow songs when I'm tired, and fast music when I work."*
- 💡 **"Same here."** — 🎯 **two words for "me too."** Used constantly in English, especially when someone admits a weakness. It makes you sound relaxed, and it takes no vocabulary at all.
- 💡 **"I read one page three times before I understand it."** — funny, specific, and true. Specific numbers (one, three, two) make any sentence memorable.
- 💡 **"A lot of different kinds."** — a natural hedge when you don't want to commit to one thing. Better than *"Everything."*
- 🎙️ Play `friend-03` and answer. **Today: say "Same here" out loud once.** It is the fastest way to agree with someone.
- 🧠 **Your mental model:** when you have no big vocabulary, **use time and contrast** instead. *"Slow when I'm tired, fast when I work"* needs only words you already have, but it sounds like a real opinion. Boring vocabulary in an interesting structure sounds better than good vocabulary in a flat sentence.
