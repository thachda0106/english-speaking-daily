# Day 28 · Conversation 5 — Someone Who Does Not Share Your Interest

## 🎬 Situation
You like something. They don't. This is a normal situation and it does not have to be awkward — unless you argue.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles), then play the audio and answer

> **Friend:** Do you watch any series? Everyone at work watches that one on Netflix.

> **You:** No, I don't watch many series. I don't have time in the week.

> **Friend:** It's only one hour. Everyone watches it.

> **You:** Maybe one day. Right now I'm reading more, I think.

> **Friend:** Oh, you read? What do you read?

> **You:** Books about technology, mostly. Nothing like a series, really.

> **Friend:** Okay. Different people, different things.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary — all words you already know
`watch · series · everyone · work · one · no · don't have time · week · only · maybe · one day · right now · reading · more · think · read · books · technology · mostly · nothing like · really · different people · different things`

## ✅ After you speak
- The not-shared formula = **polite no + a reason + find the one thing you share**:
  *"No, I don't watch many series — I don't have time in the week. Maybe one day. Do you watch anything I should see?"*
- 💡 **"Maybe one day."** — 🎯 **the perfect gentle refusal.** It says *no* without closing the door. You are not lying and you are not rejecting them. Use it whenever you are not interested but do not want to offend.
- 💡 **"Right now I'm reading more, I think."** — the *right now* + *I think* double softener. Two small hedges make a preference sound like a preference, not a rule.
- 💡 **"Different people, different things."** — 🎯 **the sentence that saves you.** It ends a disagreement without any words of conflict. Neither of you is wrong; you are just different. Every conversation needs an exit like this.
- 🎙️ Play `friend-05` and answer. **Today: say "Maybe one day" and "Different people, different things" out loud.**
- 🧠 **Your mental model:** in Vietnamese, disagreeing politely often means not saying no directly. English needs something more explicit than silence, because silence here reads as *"I didn't understand."* The English way is: **no + reason + maybe.** Three small pieces, and the other person hears respect, not rejection.
