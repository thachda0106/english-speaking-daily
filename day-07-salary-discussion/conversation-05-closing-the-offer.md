# Day 7 · Conversation 5 — Closing the Offer

## 🎬 Situation
They moved, and you landed at thirty-eight million. The last part of the conversation decides everything: say yes clearly, read the details back, and agree the start date. Do not leave anything important to memory.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles), then play the audio and answer

> **Recruiter:** We've made a decision. We can give you thirty-eight million a month, plus the bonus.

> **You:** Thank you. That's comfortable for me, and I'm happy to accept the offer.

> **Recruiter:** Great! When can you start? We need you in two weeks.

> **You:** I need two weeks for my current job. After that, I can start any day you want.

> **Recruiter:** Perfect. Can you send your notice today?

> **You:** Yes. I'll write the email today and finish my current tasks first.

> **Recruiter:** Excellent. One more thing — who should we talk to about your old job?

> **You:** My current manager is fine. I will give you his name after I tell him.

> **Recruiter:** I'll send the contract this afternoon then.

> **You:** Thank you for everything. I'm looking forward to joining the team.

## ✅ After you speak
- The closing formula = **thank you + clear yes + start date**:
  *"Thank you. That's comfortable for me, and I'm happy to accept the offer. I can start in two weeks."*
- 💡 **"That's comfortable for me."** — a yes that has weight. It says the number works, not that you had no choice. Pair it with the number: *"Thirty-eight million is comfortable for me."*
- 💡 **"I'm looking forward to joining the team."** — the standard warm ending for a call or an email. One line, then stop.
- 🧠 **Your mental model:** Closing is a **deployment**. You never deploy from memory — you read the config back: number, bonus, start date, in writing. If a value is not in the contract, it did not deploy, no matter what was said on the call.
- 🎙️ Play `recruiter-05` and answer. Then write your own acceptance sentence — number, start date, thank you — and keep it in your notes for the real offer.
