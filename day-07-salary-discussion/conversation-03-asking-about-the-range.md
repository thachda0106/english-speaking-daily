# Day 7 · Conversation 3 — Asking About the Range

## 🎬 Situation
A good recruiter will tell you the range if you ask. So ask. The range is real information: it shows you the budget before you spend your own hope on the role.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles), then play the audio and answer

> **Recruiter:** Your interview went well. The team was happy with your answers today.

> **You:** Thank you, I enjoyed the questions. May I ask about the offer?

> **Recruiter:** Of course, go ahead.

> **You:** Could you tell me the salary range for this position?

> **Recruiter:** The range is thirty-two to forty-five million a month, based on experience.

> **You:** Thank you. What usually decides where I land in that range?

> **Recruiter:** Your level, your years of experience, and the team's budget for the role.

> **You:** That's very helpful. Now I understand how you decide.

> **Recruiter:** We are a fair company. We want the number to work for both sides.

> **You:** I understand. I'd like a number that is fair for both sides too.

> **Recruiter:** That's all I can tell you today. What questions do you have for me?

> **You:** Only one — when will you be able to give me the final number?

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary — all words you already know
`interview · team · happy · answers · questions · ask · offer · salary · range · position · month · experience · thank · usually · decides · land · level · years · budget · role · understand · fair · number · both sides · helpful · tell · today · final · able · give`

## ✅ After you speak
- The ask-their-range formula = **thanks + small question + a follow-up question**:
  *"Thank you, I enjoyed the questions. Could you tell me the salary range for this position? And what usually decides where I land in that range?"*
- 💡 **"May I ask about the offer?"** — the polite door-opener for every hard question in an interview. Soft voice, end going down, not up.
- 💡 **"What usually decides where I land in that range?"** — turns a wish into a question about their process. This one is easy for them to answer honestly, so it often gets you the real answer.
- 🧠 **Your mental model:** The published range is the budget's **schema**. It exists before you arrive and tells you which values are even valid. If your number sits outside it, you are not negotiating harder — you are talking to the wrong thing.
