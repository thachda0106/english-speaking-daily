# Day 7 · Conversation 4 — Making Your Case

## 🎬 Situation
They come back with a number below your range. Most people give up right here and say "okay". The skill: say thank you, ask for a little more, and support it with facts — not feelings.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles), then play the audio and answer

> **Recruiter:** We can offer you thirty-four million a month. How does that sound?

> **You:** Thank you for the offer. Could we talk about a slightly higher number?

> **Recruiter:** I understand. Tell me why you think you should be higher.

> **You:** I have six years of backend work, and I own two services alone right now.

> **Recruiter:** That's true, but the budget for this role is limited.

> **You:** I understand the budget. Based on the market for my level, I think a higher number is fair.

> **Recruiter:** I hear you. I cannot change it here, but I can take it to my manager.

> **You:** Thank you. Would the decision be final after that?

> **Recruiter:** Yes, that is the last step. I will let you know on Friday.

> **You:** That works for me. Thanks for looking at it again.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary — all words you already know

## ✅ After you speak
- The make-your-case formula = **thank you + small ask + two facts**:
  *"Thank you for the offer. Could we talk about a slightly higher number? I have six years of backend work, and I own two services alone right now."*
- 💡 **"I own two services alone."** — *own* means you are responsible for it end to end: design, build, on-call, bugs. Say *own* instead of *work on* — it is the biggest level upgrade you can say out loud.
- 💡 **"I understand the budget."** — agree with their limit before you push past it. It takes the fight out of the room and keeps the conversation about the work, not about you.
- 🧠 **Your mental model:** Making your case is a **code review, not an argument**. A review never says "I want a higher number" — it shows the diff: years of work, services you own, what breaks if this does not change. The reviewer decides, and that is exactly why you bring evidence instead of emotion.
- 🎙️ Play `recruiter-04` and answer. Then write three real facts about yourself on paper — years, services, one result — because you will need them on the real call.
