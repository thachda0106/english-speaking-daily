# Day 7 · Conversation 1 — They Ask Your Expectations

## 🎬 Situation
You are on the phone with a recruiter about a senior backend position. Before you talk about the team or the work, they will ask one thing: your salary expectations. The skill: give a range, give the reason, keep it open.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles), then play the audio and answer

> **Recruiter:** Thanks for taking the call today. Can you tell me your salary expectations for this position?

> **You:** Of course. Based on my experience and the market, I'm looking for around thirty-five to forty million a month.

> **Recruiter:** Is that the number you need to accept an offer?

> **You:** It's my expectation, but I would like to hear more about the role first. The range is not fixed.

> **Recruiter:** What makes you say that range?

> **You:** I looked at the market for senior backend engineers with my years of experience, so I know what my skills are worth.

> **Recruiter:** Understood. Do you have any other offers on the table right now?

> **You:** Not yet. I'm talking to a few companies, but I'm looking for a team where I can stay a long time.

> **Recruiter:** That's fair. I'll write it down, and we'll talk about it again after the next step.

> **You:** Thank you. I appreciate the clear answer.

## ✅ After you speak
- The expectations formula = **range + reason + keep it open**:
  *"Based on my experience and the market, I'm looking for around thirty-five to forty million a month. It's my expectation, but the range is not fixed."*
- 💡 **"I would like to hear more about the role first."** — a polite way to say "I'm not closing the door, but I'm not signing yet." Use it whenever a number feels too early.
- 💡 **"Based on…"** — starts from facts instead of feelings. *"Based on my years of experience…"* / *"Based on the market rate for my level…"* / *"Based on the work I did last year…"*
- 🧠 **Your mental model:** A salary range is a **version range** in your API. You promise what you can support, and you name the inputs that decide the final number — level, years, skills. Nobody ships a version without knowing the input, and you should not accept an offer without knowing the input either.
