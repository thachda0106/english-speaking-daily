# Day 7 · Conversation 2 — Your Current Salary

## 🎬 Situation
The recruiter now asks what you earn today. This number becomes the starting point for every later conversation, so answer it straight — and then add one sentence that makes your situation bigger than the raw number.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles), then play the audio and answer

> **Recruiter:** Thank you for the update. Can you tell me your current salary?

> **You:** Sure. My current salary is thirty million a month, and I also get a bonus at the end of the year.

> **Recruiter:** Thank you. And what would you like in the new role?

> **You:** I'd like around thirty-five to forty million. I also want to know how the bonus works here.

> **Recruiter:** That's good to know. What do you think about your level right now?

> **You:** I started two months ago, and I already work alone on the hardest part of our system.

> **Recruiter:** I see. And where do you see yourself in three years?

> **You:** I want to be the person the team can trust with the big decisions.

> **Recruiter:** That's a good answer. Last question — is there anything about the salary you need to know from us?

> **You:** No, nothing more today. I just wanted you to know my number clearly.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary — all words you already know
`salary · current · month · bonus · end · year · role · level · started · months · alone · hardest · part · system · see · three · want · person · team · trust · decisions · good · know · think · update · sure · answer · last question · nothing · today · number · clearly`

## ✅ After you speak
- The current-salary formula = **number + one extra fact + new range**:
  *"My current salary is thirty million a month, and I also get a bonus at the end of the year. I'd like around thirty-five to forty million in the new role."*
- 💡 **"I also get a bonus at the end of the year."** — the short sentence that makes your current situation bigger than the base number. Say it even if the bonus is small.
- 💡 **"I'd like around…"** — softer than "I want around…", and it sounds like a real conversation instead of a demand. Use *I'd like* for every number you say out loud.
- 🧠 **Your mental model:** Your current salary is a **baseline snapshot**, not the final diff. A review compares old → new. If you give only the base number, the diff always looks small. One extra sentence — bonus, level, what you own — makes the diff honest.
