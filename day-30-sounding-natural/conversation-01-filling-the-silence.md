# Day 30 · Conversation 1 — Filling the Silence

## 🎬 Situation
You forgot a word. The real question is not whether this happens — it happens to everyone — but what you say next. This is the whole final exam in one conversation.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles), then play the audio and answer

> **Friend:** What did you do last weekend? I didn't hear you.

> **You:** Sorry, I didn't hear you either. I was — you know what I mean — I was at home.

> **Friend:** You stopped in the middle.

> **You:** Yeah, I lost the word. I'm looking for it... got it. Sorry.

> **Friend:** Don't worry about it. I do the same thing in my first language.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary — all words you already know
`did · last weekend · hear you · sorry · you know what I mean · at home · stopped · in the middle · yeah · lost · word · looking for it · got it · don't worry · same thing · first language`

## ✅ After you speak
- The recovery formula = **acknowledge it + keep going**:
  *"Yeah, I lost the word. I'm looking for it… got it. Sorry."*
- 💡 **"You know what I mean…"** — 🎯 **the most useful phrase in this entire course.** When you forget, when you search for a word, when you start a sentence badly: *"you know what I mean…"* gives you one second to think **and nobody reacts at all.** It sounds like thinking, not failing.
- 💡 **"I'm looking for it…"** — says out loud that the word is coming. Native speakers do this constantly. It buys you three seconds and removes all pressure.
- 💡 **"Got it."** — the natural way to show you found the word. Short, casual, and it moves the conversation forward. 🎯
- 💡 **"Don't worry about it."** — what the other person says. Hearing it from someone else teaches you that your pauses are not unusual.
- 🎙️ Play `friend-01` and answer. **Today: say "You know what I mean…" out loud five times.** It must be automatic.
- 🧠 **Your mental model:** **fluency is not the absence of mistakes. It is the absence of stopping.** Native speakers lose words, restart, and self-correct constantly — they just do it faster, and they do it **without apologising three times.** One *"you know what I mean"* and you are still talking. That is the whole skill. Everything else is detail.
