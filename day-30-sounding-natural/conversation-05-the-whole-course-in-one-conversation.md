## ✅ After you speak
- The putting-it-together formula = **warm opener + news + a real difficulty + a shared laugh + a plan**:
  *"Pretty good, thanks. Actually, I moved to a new office in March. Some days I get stuck for hours, and honestly, the English is still the hard part."*
- 💡 **"How have you been?"** — 🎯 the warmest professional opener in English. You learned this on Day 9. It asks about a *stretch of time*, so it expects a story — unlike *"How are you?"*, which expects one word and closes the door.
- 💡 **"It's been a lot, but I'm fine."** — a full answer with a contrast. This is what real speakers do: give the real thing, then soften it with *but*. It sounds honest and calm.
- 💡 **"Not here."** — two words to correct an assumption. Native speakers do this constantly and it always sounds friendly.
- 💡 **"You'll get there."** — the sentence you now need to say to yourself. You heard it, and you believed it.
- 🎙️ Play `friend-05` and answer. **Today: have one real conversation in English with no script at all.**
- 🧠 **Your mental model:** look at what that conversation actually used — *how have you been* (Day 9), *actually* (Day 30), *the hard part* (Day 6), *honestly* (Day 28), *same here* (Day 28), *get there* (Day 27). **Thirty days, and you used six days' worth of English without noticing.** That is how you know it landed: not when you can quote it, but when you cannot tell which day it came from.

## 🏁 The whole course in one list

**Part 1 — work (Days 1-10)**
1. *"Let me walk you through it."* — structure any technical answer
2. *"It depends on where the time is going."* — never start with a tool
3. *"That's a fair concern."* — accept, then explain
4. *"I've only read about it."* — honest, plus your learning pattern
5. *"What would you want me to be good at after six months?"* — your best question

**Part 2 — life (Days 11-30)**
6. *"You know what I mean…"* — buy a second when you lose a word
7. *"Sorry, let me start again."* — restart without dying inside
8. *"That's good to hear."* — keep the conversation alive
9. *"Maybe one day."* — say no without closing the door
10. *"In the end…"* — land a story instead of trailing off

**And the one that holds all of it together:**
11. 🎯 **"Different people, different things."** — it ends every disagreement, and it is true about the two halves of this course.
