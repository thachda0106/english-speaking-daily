# Day 6 · Conversation 2 — How Bad Was It?

## 🎬 Situation
The manager is still on the line. Now the second question: how many users did this hit, and did we lose anything? Managers need numbers, not feelings. The skill: give a number, a time range, and a clear yes/no on the data.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles), then play the audio and answer

> **Manager:** Thanks. Now I need the number — how many users had this problem?

> **You:** About nine hundred users, between ten o'clock and ten twenty. That is about twenty minutes.

> **Manager:** Twenty minutes is long. Did we lose any data?

> **You:** No. The data is correct. The page was slow, but every update and every request went to the database. Nothing was lost.

> **Manager:** Okay. And now the page is fast again?

> **You:** Yes. I went back to the last version at ten twenty. The response is now under one second and the monitor is quiet.

> **Manager:** Good. Do we need to tell the customer?

> **You:** I think yes. The report is for customers too. We can send a short message with the time and the cause, and say the service is normal now.

> **Manager:** Do that. Keep it short — five lines, no technical words.

> **You:** Okay. I will write it now and send it before lunch.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary — all words you already know
`users · had this problem · between · minutes · long · data · correct · slow · update · request · database · nothing lost · version · response · under one second · monitor · customer · message · time · cause · service · normal · short · technical · write · send · lunch`

## ✅ After you speak
- The impact formula = **number + time range + data yes/no + current state**:
  *"About nine hundred users, between ten and ten twenty. No data was lost. The page is fast again — under one second."*
- 💡 **"impact"** — the total damage a bug did to the business. Say the word: *"The impact was nine hundred users for twenty minutes."* Managers remember the word, not just the number.
- 💡 **"affected"** = the users a bug hit. *"Nine hundred users were affected."* Very common in reports and meetings.
- 🧠 **Your mental model:** you are writing a ticket update, not an essay. A good incident update has exactly four fields: **who was affected, for how long, what happened to the data, and what is true now.** If you cover all four, you are done.
- 🎙️ Play `manager-02` and answer. Then write a REAL 5-line customer-facing update in English about a bug you have seen — no technical words — and read it out loud.
