# Day 6 · Conversation 1 — The Manager Asks What Happened

## 🎬 Situation
Your phone rings at ten in the morning. The report page is slow and the manager wants an answer NOW. You have thirty seconds. The skill here: give the time, say if it is down or only slow, and tell the manager what you are checking right now.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles), then play the audio and answer

> **Manager:** Thach, I got an alert. The report page is slow. What happened?

> **You:** I'm looking now. It started about ten minutes ago, at ten o'clock.

> **Manager:** Is the service down?

> **You:** No, the service is running. The response is slow — about ten seconds for each request. Users can still open the page, but they must wait.

> **Manager:** Do users see an error, or is it only slow?

> **You:** Only slow. There is no error message. The page opens after ten seconds, and the users just wait.

> **Manager:** Okay. Do you know the cause yet?

> **You:** I think it is our new version. I am checking the logs and the database now. I will tell you in ten minutes.

> **Manager:** Fine. Message me when you know more, and keep me updated.

> **You:** Thank you. I will send you a message in ten minutes, even if I do not have the cause yet.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary — all words you already know
`alert · report page · slow · started · service · down · running · response · request · user · error · error message · page · opens · wait · cause · version · logs · database · minutes · tell · check · know · message · send · keep · updated · thank you`

## ✅ After you speak
- The first-30-seconds formula = **when it started + down or slow + what I am checking now**:
  *"It started at ten o'clock. The service is running, but the response is slow. I am checking the logs and the database now."*
- 💡 **"I'm looking now."** — the four words that buy you time when you have no answer yet. Say it before you panic.
- 💡 **"Even if I do not have the cause yet."** — managers calm down fast when you promise a time, not an answer. A promise with a clock is professional.
- 🧠 **Your mental model:** this is your `EXPLAIN` prompt in an incident — **time + symptom + next action**, in that order, before any story. Same structure as a health check response: what failed, since when, what are you doing.
- 🎙️ Play `manager-01` and answer. Then look at your own last alert or last broken build and say the first 30 seconds OUT LOUD, in English, in under 10 seconds of speaking time.
