# Day 3 · Conversation 5 — Small Talk at Lunch & Ending the Day

## 🎬 Situation
Lunchtime. The team invites you. Small talk is where real relationships start — and it's the part most engineers avoid. Don't. It's just simple sentences.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles), then play the audio and answer

> **Colleague:** Hey, we're going for lunch. Do you want to come?

> **You:** Yes, sure! Thanks for inviting me. Where do you usually go?

> **Colleague:** There's a good noodle place near the office. It's cheap and fast.

> **You:** Sounds good, I like noodles. Is it the one next to the coffee shop?

> **Colleague:** That's the one. You already know the area!

> **You:** A little — I walked around before my interview. Ready when you are.

> **Colleague:** Great, let's go.

> **You:** *(after lunch)* Thanks for lunch, everyone. It was really nice to meet the team. See you at the stand-up tomorrow!

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary — all words you already know
`lunch · come · inviting · usually · noodle · office · cheap · fast · sounds good · coffee · shop · area · walked · interview · ready · nice · meet · team · stand-up · tomorrow`

## ✅ After you speak
- The lunch invitation is the **easiest YES in English**: *"Yes, sure! Thanks for inviting me."* Say it with energy.
- 💡 **"Ready when you are."** = "I'm ready whenever you are" — very natural and friendly.
- 💡 **"See you tomorrow!"** — the standard goodbye to people you'll meet again. Short and warm.
