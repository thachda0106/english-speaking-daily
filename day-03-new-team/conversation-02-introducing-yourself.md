# Day 3 · Conversation 2 — Introducing Yourself at the Stand-up

## 🎬 Situation
The team lead introduces you at the morning stand-up. Everyone is looking at you. Your job: 30 seconds of speaking, warm and clear. This is the SAME 30-second intro you'll reuse at every new job.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles), then play the audio and answer

> **Team lead:** Everyone, this is Thach, our new backend developer. Thach, do you want to say hello?

> **You:** Hi everyone, nice to meet you all. I'm Thach, and I'll be working on the backend with the team.

> **Colleague:** Welcome! What were you doing before this?

> **You:** I worked as a backend developer for two years, mostly with Node.js and PostgreSQL.

> **Colleague:** Nice. Do you have any experience with our stack?

> **You:** A little — I've used similar tools. I'll need some time to learn your codebase, but I learn fast.

> **Team lead:** Perfect. We're happy to have you on the team.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary — all words you already know
`everyone · backend · developer · team · hello · working · welcome · years · Node.js · PostgreSQL · experience · stack · similar · tools · time · learn · codebase · fast · happy`

## ✅ After you speak
- This is your **30-second new-team intro** — memorize it as one block:
  *"Hi everyone, nice to meet you all. I'm Thach, and I'll be working on the backend. I was a backend developer for two years, mostly with Node.js and PostgreSQL. I need some time to learn the codebase, but I learn fast."*
- 💡 **"nice to meet you all"** — use *all* when talking to a GROUP (2+ people). For ONE person: *"nice to meet you."*
- 💡 **"I learn fast."** — a small, powerful phrase. It tells the team: don't worry, I'll be useful soon.
- 🎙️ Play `team-02`, then say the FULL 30-second intro without the paper. Time it: aim for 25–35 seconds.
