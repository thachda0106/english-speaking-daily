# Day 3 · Conversation 1 — Your First Day

## 🎬 Situation
It's your first day at the new company. The manager meets you at the door. First impressions matter — greet warmly and show you're easy to work with.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles), then play the audio and answer

> **Manager:** Hi, you must be Thach! Welcome to the team.

> **You:** Yes, that's me. Thank you for having me. It's great to be here.

> **Manager:** We're happy to have you. Did you find the office okay?

> **You:** Yes, it was easy — the map you sent was very helpful.

> **Manager:** Great. This is your desk. I'll set up your computer and accounts later today.

> **You:** Perfect, thank you. Is there anything I should do first?

> **Manager:** Just relax and explore the code. We'll have a stand-up at ten, so you can meet everyone then.

> **You:** Sounds good. I'm looking forward to it.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary — all words you already know
`team · welcome · happy · office · map · helpful · desk · computer · accounts · relax · explore · code · stand-up · meet · everyone · looking forward`

## ✅ After you speak
- 💡 **"You must be Thach."** — people say this when they already know who you are. Don't answer "yes I must be" — just say *"Yes, that's me."*
- 💡 **"I'm looking forward to it."** = "I'm excited about it and I can't wait" — the perfect positive closer for any plan.
- 💡 **"It's great to be here."** — the #1 first-day line. Say it out loud 3 times with a smile.
