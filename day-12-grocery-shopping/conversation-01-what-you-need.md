# Day 12 · Conversation 1 — What You Need

## 🎬 Situation
You're in a supermarket with no list. This is harder than it sounds, because you have to say what you want *before* you can find it.

## 🗣️ Dialogue

> **You:** Excuse me, where do you keep the rice?

> **Staff:** Aisle six, on the left.

> **You:** Thank you. And the tinned tomatoes — same aisle?

> **Staff:** Same aisle, bottom shelf. Or they're in aisle nine with the pasta sauce, if it's easier.

> **You:** That's easier, thank you.

> **Staff:** No problem.

> **You:** Sorry, one more thing — do you have any of the bag I've got? It's the green one.

> **Staff:** Yeah, we've got some by the till.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary

- **Where do you keep ___?** — the natural way to ask in a shop. Not *"Where is it?"*, which sounds like you already know
- **Same aisle?** — asking if two things are in the same place. Very useful, very common
- **or they're in aisle nine, if it's easier** — a shop assistant giving you a choice, which is worth imitating
- **do you have any of the bag I've got?** — asking for a bag when you've already got one. Slightly unusual but completely understood

## ✅ After you speak

**Formula:** *Where do you keep ___? — Aisle six. — And the ___? Same aisle?*

**"Same aisle?"** is two words and saves you walking the whole shop twice. Learn it as one fixed question.

## ✅ Done when…
- …you can ask where something is without saying *"it"*
- …you can confirm two items are in the same place in one question
- …you can thank an assistant once and move on 🎯
