# Day 18 · Conversation 1 — Asking The Price

## 🎬 Situation
You need to know what something costs. English has more ways to ask this than you would expect, and the useful one is a single word.

## 🗣️ Dialogue

> **You:** Excuse me, how much is this?

> **Staff:** Twenty-eight.

> **You:** Twenty-eight? That's more than I thought.

> **Staff:** It's the last two.

> **You:** Do you have a cheaper one?

> **Staff:** The one behind you is nineteen.

> **You:** I'll take that, thanks. Is that the final price?

> **Staff:** Yeah, including the tax.

> **You:** Great.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary

- **how much is this?** — the neutral way to ask a price. **"How much is ___?"** not *"how much this is"*
- **that's more than I thought** — a soft reaction. It says you're surprised without being difficult
- **do you have a cheaper one?** — asking for an alternative in one short sentence
- **is that the final price?** — checking whether tax is included. Worth asking in any country where prices surprise people

## ✅ After you speak

**Formula:** *how much is this? — Twenty-eight. — Do you have a cheaper one? — Is that the final price?*

⚠️ **"How much is this?"** must keep the **"is"**. *"How much this?"* is the most common price-asking error learners make — and it's the same mistake as *"it's supposed rain"* from Day 16: **dropping the little word that holds the sentence together.**

## ✅ Done when…
- …you can ask a price in one short sentence
- …you can ask for a cheaper alternative without apologising
- …you can check whether tax is included 🎯
