# Day 18 · Conversation 5 — The Budget You Actually Keep

## 🎬 Situation
Money is a topic people avoid in English. It's also the easiest way to sound adult in any conversation, because everyone has an opinion and nobody minds hearing it.

## 🗣️ Dialogue

> **Friend:** How are you doing for money this month?

> **You:** Not great, honestly. It's fine, but it's tight.

> **Friend:** Same. I look at my account and then I stop looking.

> **You:** I made a budget this year, which is humiliating to admit.

> **Friend:** That's actually responsible.

> **You:** It's humbling. I didn't realise how much I was just spending until I wrote it down.

> **Friend:** What did you cut?

> **You:** The subscriptions, mostly. And the coffee, which was embarrassing.

> **Friend:** Not the coffee.

> **You:** Not the coffee.

> **Friend:** I have to keep mine. It's the only thing in my day I enjoy.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary

- **how are you doing for money?** — the natural English question. *"doing for"* is the phrase; *"how is your money"* is not English
- **it's tight** — meaning *there's not much*. The standard word, and less blunt than *"poor"*
- **humiliating to admit** — admitting something embarrassing about yourself. **"humbling"** is slightly gentler than *"humiliating"*
- **I have to keep mine** — insisting on a small indulgence. **"I have to"** is a gentle justification, not a boast

## ✅ After you speak

**Formula:** *Not great, honestly. It's fine, but it's tight. — I made a budget, which is humiliating to admit. — Not the coffee.*

⚠️ **"Not great, but it's fine"** is the single most useful shape in this conversation. **Two clauses, opposite directions, no self-pity.** English speakers use it constantly when asked how they're managing, and it works whether the answer is *"I've saved a lot"* or *"I'm skint."*

## ✅ Done when…
- …you can ask someone about money without it being a confrontation
- …you can answer honestly with *"it's fine, but it's tight"*
- …you can defend a small pleasure with *"I have to keep mine"* 🎯
