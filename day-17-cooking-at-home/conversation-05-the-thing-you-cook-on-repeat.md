# Day 17 · Conversation 5 — The Thing You Cook On Repeat

## 🎬 Situation
You've found the only dish you make. English has a lovely phrase for exactly this, and using it makes you sound self-aware rather than unimaginative.

## 🗣️ Dialogue

> **Friend:** What are you cooking?

> **You:** The same thing I've made for six months.

> **Friend:** Which is?

> **You:** This curry my friend taught me. I can't be bothered with anything else.

> **Friend:** Is it good?

> **You:** It's fine. It's consistent, which is more important.

> **Friend:** You'd get bored of it though.

> **You:** I already have. I still make it.

> **Friend:** That's not a contradiction, that's a routine.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary

- **I can't be bothered with anything else** — British for *"I don't have the energy for alternatives"*. Very useful, very casual
- **it's consistent** — a word that sounds like a work review and works just as well about dinner
- **I already have** — for something you'd expect to say *before* a long journey; saying it about boredom lands as a joke
- **that's not a contradiction, that's a routine** — a clean, quotable line. Steal the shape: *"not a X, a Y"*

## ✅ After you speak

**Formula:** *The same thing I've made for ___. — It's fine, it's ___, which is more important. — I already have. I still make it.*

⚠️ **The structure worth taking: "That's not a X, that's a Y."**
- *That's not a contradiction, that's a routine.*
- *That's not laziness, that's a system.*
- *That's not an excuse, that's a reason.*

It reframes something negative as deliberate. Used in interviews, reviews, and arguments, it makes you sound composed.

## ✅ Done when…
- …you can say *"I can't be bothered"* without it sounding rude
- …you can describe consistency with *"it's ___ , which is more important"*
- …you can reframe with *"that's not a X, that's a Y"* 🎯
