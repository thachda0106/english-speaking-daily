# Day 22 · Conversation 2 — Saying You Don't Like It

## 🎬 Situation
You don't like something. The risk is either being vague, which sounds dishonest, or being blunt, which sounds rude. English has a middle register and it matters.

## 🗣️ Dialogue

> **Friend:** Have you tried this place?

> **You:** No. I've heard it's good.

> **Friend:** You should. It's a bit different from what you're used to.

> **You:** Looking at it, actually — no. I don't think I'd enjoy it.

> **Friend:** Why not?

> **You:** I'm not a big fan of tiny plates. I want to order one thing and eat it.

> **Friend:** That's fair. It's all sharing.

> **You:** That's not really my thing. I'd rather have a proper meal and sit down.

> **Friend:** Then you'll hate it. Good to know for the group.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary

- **I've heard it's good** — acknowledging a recommendation without committing. **"I've heard"** is the standard hedge
- **I'm not a big fan of ___** — the polite way to dislike something. **"Not a big fan of"** is not the same as hating it, and that's the point
- **that's not really my thing** — softer still, and very common. **"My thing"** means what you personally enjoy
- **that's fair** — conceding their point without changing yours

## ✅ After you speak

**Formula:** *I've heard it's good. — Looking at it, actually — no. I'm not a big fan of ___. — That's not really my thing.*

⚠️ **Three levels of dislike, in order of softness:**

| Phrase | Means |
|---|---|
| *That's not really my thing* | I'd rather not |
| *I'm not a big fan of it* | I don't enjoy it |
| *I don't like it* | neutral — not rude at all |

**"I don't like it"** is a complete, perfectly polite sentence. Learners often over-apologise here; you don't need to soften it at all. **The thing to avoid is apologising for having a preference** — *"sorry, I'm just not fond of…"* makes a normal opinion sound like a flaw.

## ✅ Done when…
- …you can decline something without insulting it
- …you can use all three levels of dislike correctly
- …you can say *"I'm not a big fan of ___"* without apologising 🎯
