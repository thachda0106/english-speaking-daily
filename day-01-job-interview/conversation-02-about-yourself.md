# Day 1 · Conversation 2 — Tell Me About Yourself

## 🎬 Situation
The interview starts. The first question is always the same: "Tell me about yourself." This conversation is YOUR most important speech — practice it until it feels natural.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles)

> **Interviewer:** Great. So, can you tell me a little about yourself?

> **You:** Sure. My name is Thach, and I'm a backend developer. I've been working at a software company for about two years, mainly building APIs and working with PostgreSQL.

> **Interviewer:** That sounds good. What made you choose backend development?

> **You:** I really enjoy working with data and systems. When I write code, I like to understand how everything works under the hood — the database, the requests, the whole flow.

> **Interviewer:** And what are you working on right now?

> **You:** Right now I'm working on a web system for our company. I'm responsible for the backend — mostly the APIs and the database design.

> **Interviewer:** I see. And why do you want to join our company?

> **You:** I'm really interested in this role because it works with distributed systems, which is where I want to grow.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary — all words you already know
`backend · developer · software · company · years · building · APIs · database · code · data · systems · under the hood · requests · flow · web · responsible · role · grow`

## ✅ After you speak
- This is your **60-second elevator pitch**. Time yourself: aim for 40–60 seconds when you speak it smoothly.
- 💡 "**I'm responsible for…**" is the key phrase for describing your job in any interview: *"I'm responsible for the backend."* / *"I'm responsible for deployment."*
- 🎙️ Record it. Listen back — is it too fast? Too slow? Fix your speed, then record again.

## ✅ Done when…
- [ ] I can say "about yourself" in under 60 seconds without reading
- [ ] I used "I'm responsible for…" out loud
- [ ] I recorded myself twice
