# Day 1 · Conversation 4 — Your Questions for Them

## 🎬 Situation
Near the end, the interviewer turns the tables: "Do you have any questions for us?" — asking questions shows you are serious. Always have 2–3 ready.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles)

> **Interviewer:** You've told us a lot about yourself. Do you have any questions for us?

> **You:** Yes, a couple. Could you tell me about the team I'd work with?

> **Interviewer:** Sure. It's a team of six — three backend, two frontend, and one QA. They're friendly, and they do code reviews for every change.

> **You:** That sounds nice. And what does a typical day look like in this role?

> **Interviewer:** Very similar to yours, I think. A stand-up in the morning, then focused work, and a code review in the afternoon.

> **You:** One more question, if it's okay. How does the team handle new ideas?

> **Interviewer:** We encourage them. If you see a problem, you can bring it up in the weekly meeting and propose a solution.

> **You:** Great. That's the kind of environment I'm looking for.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary — all words you already know
`questions · team · work with · backend · frontend · QA · friendly · code review · change · typical · role · stand-up · morning · focused · afternoon · ideas · problem · weekly · meeting · solution · environment`

## ✅ After you speak
- These 3 questions work for ANY interview. Memorize them as a set:
  1. **"Could you tell me about the team I'd work with?"**
  2. **"What does a typical day look like in this role?"**
  3. **"How does the team handle new ideas?"**
- 💡 "**if it's okay**" at the end of a question makes it very polite: *"One more question, if it's okay."*
- 🎙️ Record yourself asking all 3 questions — they must sound like questions (voice goes UP at the end).
