# Day 1 · Conversation 5 — Career Plans & Closing

## 🎬 Situation
The final question is about your future, then you close the interview politely. The closing is as important as the opening.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles)

> **Interviewer:** Great. One last question — what are your career plans going forward?

> **You:** I want to keep growing as a backend engineer. My goal is to work on bigger systems — distributed systems and high-traffic services.

> **Interviewer:** And where do you want to be in two or three years?

> **You:** I'd like to be a senior engineer who can design systems and help the team make good technical decisions. I also want to mentor junior developers.

> **Interviewer:** That's a nice goal. Do you have any questions about the hiring process?

> **You:** Yes — could you tell me about the next steps and when I might hear back?

> **Interviewer:** We have two more rounds — a technical interview and a final talk with the manager. You'll hear from us within a week.

> **You:** Perfect, thank you. I really appreciate you taking the time to meet with me today.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary — all words you already know
`career · plans · growing · backend · engineer · goal · bigger · systems · distributed · high-traffic · services · senior · design · team · technical · decisions · mentor · junior · developers · hiring · process · steps · hear back · rounds · manager · week · appreciate · time`

## ✅ After you speak
- This dialogue gives you your **career story** — the ambition answer every interviewer wants to hear.
- 💡 "**I really appreciate you taking the time to meet with me today.**" is the best closing line in interviews. Say it with a smile.
- Your first test: **say the LAST line to your mirror today** — it trains a confident exit.
- 🎙️ Record the whole conversation, then say only your lines from memory.
