# Day 1 · Conversation 3 — Your Current Project

## 🎬 Situation
The interviewer wants to know what you actually DO every day. Answer with a real project story from your work — this dialogue is the template.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles)

> **Interviewer:** Let's talk a little more about your project. What does a typical day look like for you?

> **You:** Well, the day usually starts with a stand-up meeting. I tell my team what I did yesterday and what I'm planning to do today.

> **Interviewer:** And what tools do you use?

> **You:** We use Node.js for the backend and PostgreSQL for the database. We also use Docker for local development and Git for code reviews.

> **Interviewer:** Have you solved any difficult problems recently?

> **You:** Yes. One time the system was slow because of a bad database query. I used EXPLAIN to find the problem, and I fixed it with a new index. It was a good lesson for me.

> **Interviewer:** That's interesting. How do you handle bugs in production?

> **You:** We have a process. First, we check the logs to understand the problem. Then we fix it, write a test, and deploy a new version.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary — all words you already know
`stand-up · meeting · team · yesterday · planning · tools · Node.js · backend · PostgreSQL · database · Docker · development · Git · code review · solved · difficult · problems · system · slow · query · EXPLAIN · fixed · index · lesson · bugs · production · process · logs · understand · test · deploy · version`

## ✅ After you speak
- This is the **"walk me through your work"** question — the most common technical interview question. Your real project should replace the example names.
- 💡 "**We have a process.**" is a strong answer — it shows you work like a professional, not a firefighter.
- 🎙️ Record yourself, then retell the bug story **in your own words** (a real bug you fixed, with the real fix).
