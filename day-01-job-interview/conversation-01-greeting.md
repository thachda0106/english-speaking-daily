# Day 1 · Conversation 1 — The Greeting

## 🎬 Situation
You arrive for your first interview at a new company. The interviewer meets you in the lobby and walks you to the meeting room.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles)

> **Interviewer:** Hi, it's nice to meet you. Did you find the office okay?

> **You:** Nice to meet you too. Yes, I found it easily — the directions you sent were very clear. Thank you for having me.

> **Interviewer:** No problem. Did you have any trouble with the traffic?

> **You:** Not really. I came by bus, and it took about thirty minutes. The neighborhood looks really nice.

> **Interviewer:** Good. Would you like some water or coffee before we start?

> **You:** Coffee would be great, thank you. I'm a little nervous, to be honest — this is my first interview in English.

> **Interviewer:** Don't worry, just relax and be yourself. Shall we begin?

> **You:** Yes, I'm ready.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary — all words you already know
`meet · find · office · directions · clear · traffic · bus · minutes · neighborhood · water · coffee · nervous · honest · relax · begin · ready`

## ✅ After you speak
- Say this part *out loud* three times: **"Nice to meet you too. Thank you for having me."** — this is the standard greeting line for ANY first meeting.
- 💡 **"Shall we begin?"** is a polite way to say "Let's start" — you can also use it to open a meeting at work.
- 🎙️ Record yourself reading the dialogue, then close your eyes and say only your 5 lines from memory.
