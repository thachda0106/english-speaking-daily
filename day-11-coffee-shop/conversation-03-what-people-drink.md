# Day 11 · Conversation 3 — What People Drink

## 🎬 Situation
The coffee has arrived and there is a pause. This is the moment where you find out whether you can keep a conversation going when nobody has asked you a question.

## 🗣️ Dialogue

> **You:** What do you usually drink?

> **Friend:** Just black coffee. I'm boring.

> **You:** No, that's not boring. I have a flat white every day, it's the same thing.

> **Friend:** So you do drink milk then.

> **You:** Yeah — I'd like to, but I can't. And I stopped drinking it, actually.

> **Friend:** Right. So what's your go-to order?

> **You:** Flat white, no sugar. My friend calls it my personality.

> **Friend:** That's rude, but probably true.

> **You:** What about you? Same thing every morning?

> **Friend:** Yeah. Black, no sugar. Since I was about twelve.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary

- **go-to** — the thing you always choose. Informal, and exactly what you want here
- **black coffee** — with no milk and no sugar. Not the same as *bittersweet*
- **I'd like to, but I can't** — the polite way to say something is impossible for you
- **Since I was about twelve** — starting a story without saying *"when I was young"*

## ✅ After you speak

**Formula:** *What do you usually ___? — [Your answer]. What about you?*

**"What about you?"** is the most useful question in this entire course. It's short, it's natural, and it hands the conversation straight back to the other person. Practise saying it until it comes out without thinking.

## ✅ Done when…
- …you can ask one question and it turns into three turns of talking
- …you can describe your own routine using *"every day"*, *"since I was…"*
- …you can say *"that's not boring"* instead of *"no"* 🎯
