# Day 11 · Conversation 2 — Changing Your Order

## 🎬 Situation
You order, then immediately realise you wanted something different. This happens to everyone, every day. What matters is being able to change your mind **without** feeling like a difficult person.

## 🗣️ Dialogue

> **You:** Hi. Can I get a cappuccino, please?

> **Friend:** Sure. Here you go.

> **You:** Actually — sorry, could you make that a flat white instead?

> **Friend:** No problem. One moment.

> **You:** Sorry about that.

> **Friend:** It's fine, happens all the time.

> **You:** And could you take the sugar out? I left it in by mistake.

> **Friend:** Sure. Here you are.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary

- **Actually** — the word that makes a change possible. Saying it stops you feeling embarrassed
- **Could you make that a ___ instead?** — the useful shape: name the thing, then *instead*
- **I left it in by mistake** — taking responsibility without drama
- **It's fine, happens all the time.** — how the other person forgives you, and **happens all the time** is the part worth stealing: it says *you're not unusual*

## ✅ After you speak

**Formula:** *Actually — sorry, could you make that a ___ instead?*

That is four words that cover every change order you will ever need in English.

⚠️ The mistake to avoid is apologising twice: *"Sorry, actually, could I change it — sorry."* Once is enough. **"Sorry" belongs to the change, not to you.**

## ✅ Done when…
- …you can change an order with one sentence and no apology spiral
- …you can accept being forgiven without thanking three times 🎯
