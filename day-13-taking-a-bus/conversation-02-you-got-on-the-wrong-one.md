# Day 13 · Conversation 2 — You Got On The Wrong One

## 🎬 Situation
You are on the bus. You realise you are going the wrong way. This has happened to everyone, and how you handle it decides how much of the next hour is unpleasant.

## 🗣️ Dialogue

> **You:** Excuse me — this is the wrong bus, isn't it?

> **Driver:** Where did you want to go?

> **You:** To the station. I thought this one went there.

> **Driver:** It does — but not until after the industrial estate.

> **You:** Right. Can I get off here and change?

> **Driver:** There's a stop right here, and one goes back in about eight minutes.

> **You:** This one's fine, thank you.

> **Driver:** No worries. You'll see the station on the sign.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary

- **this is the wrong bus, isn't it?** — a question that gives the other person a chance to confirm. Far calmer than an accusation
- **I thought this one went there** — **"I thought"** is doing real work here. It admits your mistake without you having to say *"I was wrong"*
- **Can I get off here and change?** — asking permission rather than just jumping off. *"Can I…"* rather than *"I'll get off"*
- **this one's fine, thank you** — accepting a correction. **"No worries"** is the reply you'll hear most

## ✅ After you speak

**Formula:** *this is the wrong bus, isn't it? — Where did you want to go? — I thought this one went there. — Can I get off here?*

⚠️ **"I thought…"** is the most useful phrase in this conversation. It lets you be wrong without apologising twice.

## ✅ Done when…
- …you can discover a mistake out loud without panicking
- …you can say *"I thought"* instead of *"I was wrong"*
- …you can ask a driver a question mid-journey 🎯
