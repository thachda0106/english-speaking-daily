# Day 9 · Conversation 5 — Ending the Lunch and Going Back

## 🎬 Situation
Lunch is over. This is the moment most people find hardest — you run out of things to say and it feels awkward. The skill is a **clean exit**. Small talk has rules: **notice, wrap, leave**.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles), then play the audio and answer

> **Colleague:** That was really good. I think I'll come here again next week.

> **You:** Yeah, me too. Thanks for telling me about this place.

> **Colleague:** No problem. Oh, it's already one o'clock, huh.

> **You:** Right, we should go back before the meeting starts.

> **Colleague:** True. See you this afternoon then.

> **You:** See you! Have a good one.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary — all words you already know
`really good · come here again · next week · me too · thanks · telling me · no problem · already · o'clock · go back · before · meeting · starts · true · see you · afternoon · have a good one`

## ✅ After you speak
- The exit formula = **notice the time + say we should go + small goodbye**:
  *"Right, we should go back before the meeting starts. See you this afternoon!"*
- 💡 **"It's already one o'clock, huh."** — the *huh* at the end makes it a soft observation, not a complaint. This is very natural English. 💡 *small talk ends when someone notices the time* — you don't need a big goodbye.
- 💡 **"Me too."** — two words that mean "I agree and I feel the same." Extremely useful. *"I love that place." — "Me too."*
- 💡 **"Have a good one."** — short, friendly, and it works with almost anyone. Not as formal as "Have a good afternoon."
- 🎙️ Play `colleague-05` and answer. **Today: leave one lunch with "Have a good one" instead of just a wave.**
