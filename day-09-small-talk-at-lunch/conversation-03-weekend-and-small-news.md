# Day 9 · Conversation 3 — Weekend and Small News

## 🎬 Situation
The food is coming. Now you need to keep talking. The easiest topic is **the weekend** — everyone has one, and it's always safe. The skill is **going deeper**: one detail and one question back.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles), then play the audio and answer

> **Colleague:** So, what did you do last weekend?

> **You:** Not much, honestly. I stayed home and watched a few videos. And you?

> **Colleague:** I went hiking with my girlfriend. It was really hot, but the view was great.

> **You:** That sounds fun. How far did you walk?

> **Colleague:** About ten kilometers. My legs were sore for two days!

> **You:** Ten kilometers? That is a lot. I think I would need a week to recover.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary — all words you already know
`weekend · honestly · stayed home · watched · videos · hiking · girlfriend · hot · view · great · far · walk · kilometers · legs · sore · two days · recover · that sounds fun`

## ✅ After you speak
- The keep-it-going formula = **answer + one detail + ask them back**:
  *"Not much, honestly. I stayed home and watched a few videos. And you?"*
- 💡 **"Not much, honestly."** — very natural, and it stops you from inventing a good story. Short answers + honesty are easy to say.
- 💡 **"That sounds fun."** — the best three-word reaction in English. It works for anything: a trip, a hobby, a plan. Say it and then ask a follow-up question.
- 💡 **"I think I would need a week to recover."** — this is how you show personality without a big vocabulary. *I think…* + *I would…* keeps you honest and sounds natural.
- 🎙️ Play `colleague-03` and answer. **Today: ask a coworker one "What did you do last weekend?"** and ask one follow-up question.
- 🧠 **Your mental model:** small talk is not a test, it's a **ping-pong**. You throw the ball, they throw it back. The only mistake is catching the ball and holding it. Answer + ask = throw it back.

## ✅ Done when…

- [ ] I asked a follow-up question (not just "And you?")
