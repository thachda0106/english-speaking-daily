# Day 10 · Conversation 5 — Your Questions and the Close

## 🎬 Situation
The technical part is over. Now you ask the questions — and this is where many people say "no, I'm fine" and lose points. Asking good questions shows you are already thinking about their problem. And you always close by **repeating what you heard**.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles), then play the audio and answer

> **Interviewer:** That covers the technical side. Do you have any questions for us?

> **You:** Yes, two things. First, what does the team look like right now, and how is the work split between backend and frontend?

> **Interviewer:** Good question. We have four backend engineers and two frontend, and we work in small teams of two or three.

> **You:** Okay, and the second question — for this role, what would you want me to be good at after six months?

> **Interviewer:** That's a nice question. I'd want you to be able to own a whole feature by then, including the database design.

> **You:** That sounds like a good challenge. One last thing — what do you enjoy most about working here?

> **Interviewer:** Honestly, the people. The problems are interesting, and everyone is willing to help. That's rare.

> **You:** I can hear that, and it sounds like a good place to work. Thank you for your time today.

## ✅ After you speak
- The closing formula = **team question + growth question + human question + thank you**:
  *"What does the team look like? And for this role, what would you want me to be good at after six months?"*
- 💡 **"What would you want me to be good at after six months?"** — 🎯 **the best question in this course.** It makes them describe the job, shows you're serious, and lets you find out if the role is real. Ask it in **every** interview.
- 💡 **"What do you enjoy most about working here?"** — asking about their *experience* is warm, and it often gives you real information about the team. 💡 A good manager will say something honest here. Listen to it.
- 💡 **"I can hear that, and it sounds like a good place to work."** = "from what you're saying, I understand". It closes with warmth. Then thank them.
- 🎙️ Play `interviewer-05` and answer. **Today: write your 3 questions down. Memorise them. Never walk out without asking.**
- 🧠 **Your mental model:** 🎯 **an interview is a two-way test.** They are checking if you can do the job; you are checking if you want this job. The candidates who ask the best questions get the better offers, because they sound like people who are already on the team.

## 🎁 The whole course in one list — your interview toolkit

1. **60-second summary** — role, years, stack, your strong area
2. **"What would you like to focus on today?"** → answer it, always
3. **"Let me walk you through it."** → situation → find → change → result
4. **"How did you measure it?"** → numbers, not opinions
5. **"It depends on where the time is going."** → never start with a tool
6. Name the **cost** of your choice — every design answer
7. **"I've only read about it"** + your learning pattern — read, build small, get feedback
8. **"What would you want me to be good at after six months?"** — your best question

> Read this list before every interview. It is the whole 10 days, compressed. 💡
