# Day 10 · Conversation 1 — Introducing Your Background

## 🎬 Situation
The technical interview starts with the easy warm-up: who are you? Almost everyone answers this badly — too short, or too long. The skill is a **60-second professional summary** that ends with a bridge to your strongest topic.

## 🗣️ Dialogue — read out loud (both roles), then play the audio and answer

> **Interviewer:** Thanks for coming. So, can you tell me a little about your background?

> **You:** Of course. I'm a backend engineer with about four years of experience, mostly with Node.js and TypeScript. I work with PostgreSQL, and I've spent a lot of time on performance — slow queries, indexes, and caching.

> **Interviewer:** Okay. And what kind of projects have you worked on?

> **You:** Mostly e-commerce and task management systems. Right now I work on a system that handles thousands of requests every minute, so I think reliability is a big part of my job.

> **Interviewer:** That sounds relevant to us. What would you like to focus on today?

> **You:** I'd be happy to talk about how I found and fixed a production performance problem. That's probably my strongest area.

## 🧠 Your active vocabulary — all words you already know
`background · backend · experience · mostly · PostgreSQL · performance · slow · queries · indexes · caching · projects · e-commerce · task management · thousands · requests · minute · reliability · relevant · focus · production · problem · strongest`

## ✅ After you speak
- The 60-second-summary formula = **role + years + core stack + one area you own**:
  *"I'm a backend engineer with about four years of experience, mostly with Node.js and TypeScript. I work with PostgreSQL, and I've spent a lot of time on performance."*
- 💡 **"What kind of projects have you worked on?"** — always asked. Answer with **domain + scale**, not a list of tools: *"e-commerce and task management systems, handling thousands of requests per minute."*
- 💡 **"What would you like to focus on today?"** — 🎯 **this is the most important question of the day.** They are giving you permission to choose your best topic. **Always answer it.** Never say "whatever you want to ask."
- 💡 **"I'd be happy to…"** = *I would be glad to.* Polite and confident. Use it every time they hand you the floor.
- 🎙️ Play `interviewer-01` and answer. **Today: record your 60-second summary on your phone and listen to it. That recording is your interview intro.**
- 🧠 **Your mental model:** the interviewer's real question is never "what tools do you use?" It's **"can this person think clearly and explain it?"** The summary is your chance to set the topic before they set it for you.
